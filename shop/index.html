Our studio

Two potters, one wheel, and a lot of clay.

Kiln & Co. started in a shared garage studio with a second-hand wheel and a kiln that took three tries to fire properly. Today we make around 200 pieces a month — still by hand, still in small batches.

We glaze in autumn colours because they look even better with coffee stains. Every piece carries tiny marks from the making: that's the point.
