FAQ

Common questions

Can you take over from my current accountant?

Yes. We handle the handover and request your records directly, usually within two weeks.

Do I need to use accounting software?

We recommend it, and we'll set it up and train you for free on Growth and Complete plans.

Do you work with clients outside Leicester?

Yes, about half our clients are across the UK. We work by video call and secure upload.

What happens if HMRC gets in touch?

We deal with HMRC on your behalf, and simple enquiries are covered by your plan.
