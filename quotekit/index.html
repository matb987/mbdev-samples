How to add it

Most website builders have a “custom code” or “embed” block.

  1. Copy the code aboveIt's the same code for every page.
  2. Add an embed block to your pagePut it where the price box should go, for example next to your main heading.
  3. Paste, save and publishThe widget uses your site's font and your brand colour, so it looks like part of your site.

What it does

  • Shows a price range straight away, using your rules
  • Checks the postcode against your areas and adds travel charges
  • Collects name, email and best time, then sends you the lead
  • Styles are sealed off, so it can't clash with your theme
  • Lightweight (about 7 KB compressed), no cookies, no tracking

Beta note: in this demo, price rules and leads are saved in your browser. A live version would store them in your account.
