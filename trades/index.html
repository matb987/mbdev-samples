FAQ

Common questions

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Quotes for new boilers and larger jobs are always free. For repairs we charge a fixed call-out, which includes the first hour of labour.

How quickly can you come out?

For emergencies (no heating, no hot water, or an active leak) we aim to be with you within two hours. Most other jobs are booked within 1–2 days.

Can I spread the cost of a new boiler?

Yes — monthly payment options are available on new boiler installs, subject to status.

Are your engineers qualified?

All our engineers are fully qualified and registered for gas work, and every job is covered by our public liability insurance.
