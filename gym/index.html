FAQ

Good questions

I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Most members start as beginners. Your free session includes a movement check so we can scale every class to you.

What should I bring?

Comfy clothes, trainers and a water bottle. We've got towels, chalk and lockers.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes, for up to 2 months a year for holidays or injury, free of charge.

Is there parking?

Free on-street parking after 6pm, and we're 4 minutes' walk from Exhibition Centre station.

First session's on us.

Meet a coach, try a class, no pressure to join.
