Questions, answered

Is my data safe?

Bank connections are read-only via regulated Open Banking providers, data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we never sell your information.

Can I move from my current software?

Yes. Import your chart of accounts, contacts and opening balances from a spreadsheet or your existing tool in a few minutes.

Do I still need an accountant?

Tallyflow keeps your books tidy day-to-day. Most customers still use an accountant for year-end — and they get free access.

What happens after the free trial?

You choose a plan, or your account simply pauses. We never charge without asking first.
